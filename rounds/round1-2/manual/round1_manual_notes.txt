Round 1 Manual Challenge: "An Intarian Welcome"

MECHANIC: Exchange Auction
- Two products: DRYLAND_FLAX and EMBER_MUSHROOM
- You submit a single limit order (price, quantity) per product — you are a BUYER (bidder)
- You submit LAST. No other orders arrive after yours. Order book is fully known.

AUCTION CLEARING RULES
1. Exchange picks a single clearing price that maximizes total traded volume
2. Ties broken by choosing the higher price
3. All bids with price >= clearing price AND asks with price <= clearing price execute at the clearing price
4. Allocation: price priority, then time priority
5. You are LAST in time priority at any price level you join (worst priority at your price)

GUARANTEED BUYBACK (Post-Auction)
- DRYLAND_FLAX: buyback at 30 per unit, no fees
- EMBER_MUSHROOM: buyback at 20 per unit, fee: 0.10 per unit traded

PROFIT FORMULA
- DRYLAND_FLAX: profit = (30 - clearing_price) * quantity_filled
- EMBER_MUSHROOM: profit = (20 - clearing_price - 0.10) * quantity_filled = (19.90 - clearing_price) * quantity_filled

STRATEGY CONSIDERATIONS
1. For each possible bid price, simulate the clearing price and your fill quantity
2. Since you're last in time priority, at the clearing price level you only get residual volume
3. Your bid can SHIFT the clearing price (adding bid volume changes max-volume calculation)
4. Optimize (bid_price, bid_quantity) to maximize profit = (buyback - clearing_price) * your_fill
